4753-softcopy — the three files SIDdecompiler could not disassemble
===================================================================

Date:   2026-07-31 (batch32)
Method: scripts/dev/sid2prg.js -> retro_load -> retro_disassemble.
        No SIDdecompiler involvement. All three disassembled first try.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. The 4753-softcopy card reached `verified` covering only
32 of its 34 $1000-convention files. Three files were left undisassembled
because SIDdecompiler hangs on them, and the card carried an explicitly
unresolved question about the third: whether the cross-composer file genuinely
reuses $4753's routine or is a looser Player-ID tag match, "flagged, not
asserted" across several passes.

Both questions are now answered. All three are the same player.

SCOPE: static disassembly only (isExecuted=false throughout). Nothing was
executed, reassembled, byte-diffed or trace-diffed. The card's `verified`
status and its 32-of-34 scope are NOT extended on the strength of this — what
is established here is structural identity, not a register-write match.
